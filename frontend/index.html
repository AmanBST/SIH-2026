Government rules used for packaged commodity inspection

CENTRAL ACT

Legal Metrology Act, 2009

The principal legislation governing standards of weights and measures and related legal metrology requirements in India.

Government of India 57 Sections
KEY RULES

Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011

Rules governing declarations and requirements applicable to pre-packaged commodities.

2011 Amendments included
PACKAGED COMMODITIES

Mandatory Declarations

Information that may be required on packages under the applicable rules.

01

Name & Address

Name and address of the manufacturer, packer or importer.

Country of Origin

Country of origin for imported products.

Rule 6

Net Quantity

Net quantity expressed in the standard unit of weight, measure or number.

Date Information

Month and year of manufacture and applicable date declarations.

How these rules are used

LegalScan extracts declarations from the product label using OCR and compares the extracted information against the applicable compliance rules. Each finding can be linked to its relevant rule for explainable inspection results.
